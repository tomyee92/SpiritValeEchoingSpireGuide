How to read this sheet

The one rule

Melee and Ranged are stopped by DEF. Magic is stopped by MDEF. Every boss's damage is split between the two, so the swap call is just: whichever side is bigger.

MDEF wins the climb

Most Spire bosses deal chiefly Magic damage, so MDEF is your default kit. Swap to DEF only on the floors marked DEF below.

Curse is the priest trap

Under Curse, your heals damage you. It shows as a Curse flag in a boss's tooltip — check before you commit a healer.

Umbral Fragment

Clearing a floor earns Umbral Fragment — more the higher you climb.
