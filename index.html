The Echoing Spire

A floor-by-floor climb sheet for all 20 boss floors, 5 through 101. For each one: what armor to wear, what element to resist, and what will lock you out of the fight.

Damage figures are modeled estimates of composition — what share of a boss's output is which element and which type — not the raw numbers it hits you for. Treat them as planning guidance. Game build —.

How to read this sheet

The one rule

Melee and Ranged are stopped by DEF. Magic is stopped by MDEF. Every boss's damage is split between the two, so the swap call is just: whichever side is bigger.

MDEF wins the climb

Most Spire bosses deal chiefly Magic damage, so MDEF is your default kit. Swap to DEF only on the physical floors — they are marked DEF below.

Curse is the priest trap

Under Curse, your heals damage you. Check the Curse floors before you climb — filter for them with the Curse chip.

Umbral Fragment

Clearing a floor earns Umbral Fragment — more the higher you climb.

    Know before you climb

    Silence Priests can't heal

    Up to 5s at 100%. Pre-buff, carry Silence resist, or burst them down.

    Freeze 10 seconds of nothing

    Frozen means cannot act. Freeze immunity is near-mandatory on these.

    Stun Short but constant

    Mostly 3s, often at 100% per landed cast, and often stacked several to a boss.

    Curse Heals turn on you

    The single most run-ending debuff for a healer-led party.
